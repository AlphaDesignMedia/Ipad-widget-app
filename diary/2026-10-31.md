# Saturday 31 October · "slow and steady"

**What happened**
- Pause all proved the stutters were plugins, not the app. The backbone held, and Glass Clock was never touched.
- Glass Memory and Window Cleaner were made light, then parked.
- The dashboard became a Glass Clock tile on the canvas, and "Needs you" got answer buttons.
- The team grew: Robyn (core lead), Tess the Tester, Mappy, and Peter and Monty in the background.
- The Plugin Kit started: the checker now reads every plugin on every push.
- Decided: a TestFlight beta, with the Dad test as the north star.

**Pictures:** img/2026-10-31-mind-map.jpg (Aaron's mind map: what Glass Clock is), img/2026-10-31-world-map.png (how it's made)

**Lessons for the next app**
1. Rushing broke more than it fixed. One change at a time.
2. Keep a backbone the experiments can't break, and a big "pause everything" switch.
3. AI testing isn't people testing.
4. Write the principles down (Peter's Book) before the business pressure arrives.
