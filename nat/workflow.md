```
  new idea 💡 ──▶ 🌱 Idea garden (design partner) or the dashboard's idea shelf
```

## How the agents work on their own

- Only on jobs that are already **briefed and approved**.
- Every job ends with: **did · didn't test · what's next**, one line each, in `AGENT-NOTES.md`.
- The stable build stays stable. Experiments live in plugins, behind a switch, or in the 🗄 drawer.
- Anything that crashes, freezes or slows: park it, write a ticket, carry on (the existing rule).
- Stuck or going round in circles: stop and ask Aaron.
