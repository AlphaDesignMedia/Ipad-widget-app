# 🪶 Pip: design decisions · source of truth (Aaron, 1 Nov 2026)

**Aaron's own drawings are the authority** for Pip's proportions, silhouette, anatomy, expressions, wings and
personality. AI images and older concepts are ideas only. Translate the drawings (3D, glass, motion); don't replace them.

## The rules
1. Drawings first: Sheets A and B (Procreate, 31 Oct) and anything Aaron draws next.
2. Don't carry over old decisions (traced V2 ears, Drop look, pigeon morph, AI boards) unless they're in his drawings.
3. **Unsure about a shape, move, face, wing or proportion? Stop and ask Aaron for a drawing.** Don't guess.
4. Flag every detail the builder invented. List it on the page itself.
5. Experiments stay separate from approved decisions. Nothing becomes "Pip" without Aaron's yes.
6. **Never phallic or sperm-like.** No thin tail trailing a round head. Keep it chunky and soft. Movement is lean,
   tip, squash and stretch (Sheet A), not a whipping tail.
