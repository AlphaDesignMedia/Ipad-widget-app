# Dashboard queue

Ideas waiting to go on Aaron's real dashboard. The Dashboard queue plugin (Dashboard panel) walks through moving them over. One item per heading; the text under each is the paste.

## Pip's Sketchbook

💡 Pip's Sketchbook: Pip loves watching you draw, wants to join, wishes they were better. Starts thinking everything is a house or a face. Stages: Watch (built) → Guess, you teach → Trace Pip's wobbly drawing → skill grows. Rules: Pip off = no effect; offers 3 times; Pip's lines on their own layer; learning stays on the device.

## Pip's name drifts

💡 Pip's name drifts: everyone starts as Pip. Over weeks the vowel follows their personality: Pip curious, Pep energetic, Pop playful, Pap calm, Pup cheeky. A mini you with their own quirks. Slow, Pip asks first, lockable, on device, Pip off = none.

## Circles over sliders

💡 Circles over sliders: 2D pads for Pip's personality, 4 labelled edges, a live readout, Pip reacts while you drag. Fix Helper Dial the same way.

## Help Pip home

💡 Help Pip home: tap to flap a tired, heavy Pip to a ledge. Pip's own game: no pipes, no fail screen.

## Pip's backstory

💡 Pip's backstory: once the star of "Updraft", a forgotten made-up arcade cabinet. Flew out through the glass when the arcade closed. Now flaps by choice, loves rests and being carried.

## Pip's story unfolds

💡 Pip's story unfolds: chapters unlock with time and the Bond ladder, never money. Branches from how you use the app. Light users get the cover story: "I was a screensaver."

## Android

💡 Android: no port for now. Try the web Pip Sandbox in Chrome on Android (Add to Home Screen).

## Restraint ladder

💡 Restraint ladder: Play → Proven → Hidden → Gentle (off by default) → Core. First open of Glass Clock = just the clock.

## Busy Pip

💡 Busy Pip: in Developer mode, Pip with a clipboard audits the plugins. Real mode = clear and exact, 3 questions max. Pretend mode = confident nonsense. Your answers set how calm Pip is for everyone.

## Tea break

💡 Tea break: hard-working Pip asks for a cuppa 3 times max, then makes their own and sulks a little.

## Guest Takeover

💡 Guest Takeover: made-up guests (Tack the drawing pin, Fuse the lamp) barge in and do Pip's job. Pip takes it back.

## Pip checks themselves

💡 Pip checks themselves: after each update Pip tries what's new and asks "Still feel like me?". Feels off = that change switches off, plus a note for the developer. 3 per build.

## Feel rule

💡 Feel rule: every build must pass "Does this still feel like Pip?": smooth, fresh, fits a floating fairy, one tap off.
