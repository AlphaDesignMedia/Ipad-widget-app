# New app template

Copy this for each new app.

1. **One sentence:** what does it do and what does it replace?
2. **Why is it better than what's out there?** (no ads, cleaner, simpler)
3. **v1.0 scope:** one thing. Everything else goes to v1.1.
4. **Copy the `Launch/` folder** and replace the names.
5. **Reuse:** developer account, privacy policy structure, support page, screenshot style.
6. **Milestones:** Setup → Prototype → Customiser/Core → Polish → Publish
7. **Done means:** live on the App Store, and you used it yourself for a week.
