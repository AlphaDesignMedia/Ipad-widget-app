# Glass apps: brand brief (draft, 22 Oct 2026)

From the owner's note on the dashboard: "an elegant design tool to create the lock screen or widget of your dreams: something you can use to match your lock screen and build the theme you really want." The brand should be ad-free and generous with the basics, with a premium version that has lots of cool extras and stays really cheap.

## The promise
**Make your Home Screen look like it was designed for you.** Glass Clock is the first piece: a clock widget you style to match your lock screen and your theme. Later Glass apps add more pieces in the same style.

## How it should feel
- **Elegant but technical:** a designer's dream tool. Calm on the surface, with precise control underneath.
- **Apple-native:** Liquid Glass, real contrast, and Apple's design guidelines followed with care. It never looks like a theme pack.
- **Playful in the moments that matter:** moods, the glow as a choice lands, a panel with weight. Never noisy.

## Two ways in
- **Classic:** the steady, reliable path. It must always work perfectly.
- **Panels:** the studio. More playful and experimental, for people who love design.

## Voice
Short, warm and plain. We talk about what you'll see, not how it works. No hype, no exclamation marks in the app, no dark patterns.

**Tagline options**
1. Glass Clock: the widget of your dreams.
2. Match your lock screen. Love your Home Screen.
3. Your Home Screen, designed by you.
4. Set the mood.

## The deal with people
- **No ads, no tracking, ever.**
- **Generous free:** every preset and mood, Feeling Lucky, every size, the true preview and Apply to Widget. Every accessibility feature, always.
- **Pro, once, cheaply (£1.99–£2.49):** the fine controls, your own photos and backdrops, saved styles, and the Panels studio. You can try anything first; only applying it needs Pro.
- **Tips stay optional:** the drinks menu, never a nag.
