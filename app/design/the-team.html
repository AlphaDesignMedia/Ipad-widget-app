🎟 When something breaks: the ticket route

Up to now, plugin problems got fixed in the Terminal. From today, plugins get fixed in the Yard and the Workshop only reports them:

💥you see a crash, freeze or slowness
→
🏭the Workshop parks the plugin and writes a ticket
→
🎨Jules fixes it in the Yard
→
✅you say "bring it back"

🖥 Terminal step (paste once, and Greg keeps it as a rule)

New rule from Aaron: plugins are fixed by the cloud plugin agent (Jules), not here. When a plugin crashes, freezes or makes the app slow:
1) Park it: comment out its line in Plugins.swift with "// parked: , ticket ".
2) Write a ticket at the top of TICKETS.md (create it if missing): the date, the plugin id, what Aaron saw in his own words, how to make it happen, any crash log lines, and what you parked.
3) Commit and push to pip-playground.
Don't change the plugin's own code; Jules fixes it on pip-plugins and answers under the ticket. Core problems (host, plugin bar, panels, Pip) are still yours. Add this rule to CLAUDE.md under "Who does what".

I check TICKETS.md every time I start work. If you want something fixed straight away, just say "Jules, ticket" to me.
