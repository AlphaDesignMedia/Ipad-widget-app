How it works

Each shape is just circles, like a logo grid. The outline wraps Body, Head and Tail with straight lines between, and the Tip wraps onto the head as a point (Pip's tip, the pigeon's beak). Cut ⊖ bites a curve out (the chin).

👆 Drag a circle's middle to move it, its edge ring to resize it. Shrink one to nothing for a sharp point. Double-tap Cut's ring to switch it on or off. Eyes: drag, or double-tap to hide. Squares are the leg: drag, or double-tap to tuck it away.

In the vector morph each circle slides to its twin: Pip's tip becomes the beak.
