How it works

Both shapes share the same 14 numbered dots. In the vector morph, dot 1 slides to dot 1, and so on. The dashed line is the other shape.

👆 Drag a dot to reshape. Double-tap a dot to switch it between smooth and a sharp corner (◆), for the tip and the beak. Rings are the eyes: drag them, or double-tap one to hide it (side-on pigeons show one eye). The two squares are the leg's hip and foot: drag them, or double-tap to tuck the leg away.
