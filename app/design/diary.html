Saturday 31 October

"Slow and steady"

📌 What happened

  • Pause all proved the stutters were plugins, not the app. The backbone held.
  • Glass Memory and Window Cleaner were made light, then parked.
  • The dashboard became a Glass Clock tile on the canvas.
  • The team grew: Robyn 💜, Tess 🧪, Mappy 🗺, Peter 🧢, Monty 💼.
  • The Plugin Kit's checker went live.
  • Decided: a TestFlight beta, with the Dad test as the north star.

🎓 Lessons for the next app

  1. Rushing broke more than it fixed. One change at a time.
  2. Build a backbone the experiments can't break, plus a big pause switch.
  3. AI testing isn't people testing.
  4. Write the principles down before the business pressure arrives.

🔎 Findings

  • A friend's favourite part was drawing with Pip. It's a hook, not a new app.
  • Older users flicking Pip away is fine, and it should be easy.

Friday 30 October

"The show-off"

📌 What happened

  • A stable show-off build with everything on, plus a Tuck away button.
  • Drop Pip designed: two eyes, curvy, wings from the back.
  • A sketch sheet for drawing new Pips.
  • The show-off went really well.

🎓 Lessons

  1. Pushing from an old copy wiped other people's work. Always check first.
  2. Panicking about stability led to rushing, and rushing led to more instability.

🔎 Findings

  • One eye on Pip is unsettling. Two eyes are friendly.
  • Silhouette matters: a circle reads, and the drop adds something extra.
