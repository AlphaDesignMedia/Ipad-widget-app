## Support page text

# Glass Clock support

**How do I add the widget?**
Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Glass Clock.

**My changes aren't showing on the widget.**
Open Glass Clock once, adjust a setting, then go back to the Home Screen. The widget updates within a few seconds.

**The widget looks different on a Clear or Tinted Home Screen.**
iPadOS redraws widgets in its own glass or tint on those styles. The app has options for keeping your colours where your iPadOS version allows it.
