## Description
Glass Clock puts a clean, good-looking clock on your iPad Home Screen.

I made it because the clock apps I tried were full of ads and clutter. This one does one thing: it shows the time, and it looks good doing it.

HOW IT WORKS
Open the app, style your clock with a live preview, then add the Glass Clock widget to your Home Screen.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Presets to start from
• Choose from several fonts
• Adjust text style and transparency
• Add a soft glow or shadow
• Glass or solid card, with adjustable transparency
• 12 or 24-hour time, with optional AM/PM and date
• Works in small, medium, large and extra large widget sizes
• Looks right in Light, Dark, Clear and Tinted Home Screens

NO ADS, NO ACCOUNTS, NO TRACKING
Glass Clock collects no data and never connects to the internet.

Requires iPadOS 26 or later.

## Notes for App Review
> Glass Clock is a Home Screen widget app for iPad. To see the widget: long-press the Home Screen, tap Edit, Add Widget, choose Glass Clock, and pick a size. Settings made in the app update the widget. The app has no login, no network use and collects no data.
